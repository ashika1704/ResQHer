
# ResQHer — A Campus Safety Prototype

**Tagline:** We don't wait for the SOS. We watch for the silence.

## About the Project

ResQHer is a student-developed campus safety prototype exploring how technology can help improve safety awareness, emergency support, and access to assistance in campus environments.

The project demonstrates an early-stage concept and is being developed as part of a student innovation initiative.

## Problem Statement

Students may face unsafe situations on or around campus and may not always be able to seek help immediately. Traditional emergency approaches can depend on a person actively initiating an alert.

## Proposed Solution

ResQHer explores a technology-assisted safety layer designed around:

- Accessible emergency support
- Safety awareness and risk communication
- A user-friendly safety interface
- Future integration of intelligent alerts and campus support workflows

## Current Prototype

This repository contains the initial prototype and its available frontend implementation.
